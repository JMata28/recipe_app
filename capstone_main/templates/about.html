About DishData

Finding recipes online is easier than ever, but navigating cluttered pages and inconsistent formatting can make the experience frustrating.

DishData solves this by providing a clean, social platform where users can create accounts, share recipes, rate dishes, save favorites, and discover new meals—all presented in a consistent, standardized format. Every recipe follows the same clear structure, making information easy to find and compare. In addition, DishData offers AI-generated recipe suggestions in the same format, which users can freely edit and customize before saving or sharing.
